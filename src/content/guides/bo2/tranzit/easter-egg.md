Written up from MrRoflWaffles' no-nonsense video guide above — timestamps in the
headings point at the matching section of the video.

## Before you start

- **Players:** 2–4, co-op only on console. Solo is possible **on PC only** via the
  Plutonium launcher + a solo mod (links in the video description) — see the solo
  notes at the bottom.
- **Two sides:** you complete either the **Maxis** or the **Richtofen** side, not
  both. The prep below is shared; the paths split after the power step.
- You do **not** need the NavCard bench to finish this easter egg. It only matters
  later for the Buried super easter egg.

## Shared prep

### Build the turbine (0:50)

Three parts, all in the spawn room (bus depot). One part per player at a time —
craft each at the buildable table before grabbing the next:

1. **Mannequin** — leaning against the bus route map.
2. **Rudder** — on the ground next to the M14.
3. **Fan** — on the chair to the right of the buildable table.

Once crafted, every player in the game can pick up their own turbine.

### Optional: Galvaknuckles (1:40)

Strongly recommended — they one-shot the Denizens that jump you in the fog. Find
the **hatch**, which spawns in one of four spots behind turbine-powered doors:
the bus depot, the diner (by the lamp), the farm barn, or the town corner. Bring
the hatch to the **diner**, place it on the roof overhang, climb up, and buy the
Galvaknuckles for **6,000 points**.

### Turn on the power (2:33)

At the power station, drop down into the power area and collect three parts for
the power switch (each has multiple spawn points):

1. **Board** — left wall right after you drop down, against the center boxes, or
   leaning against the wall inside the switch room.
2. **Hand** — on the ground right of the stairs past the entrance, right of the
   switch room, or in front of the switch room door.
3. **Switch** — right of the entrance against boxes, or on one of the two tables
   inside the switch room.

Craft at the bench in the switch room and flip the power. The **Avogadro** (the
electric monster) escapes containment — that's supposed to happen, and it's where
the two paths split.

## Maxis side (4:17)

1. With power on and the Avogadro loose, **flip the power switch back off**. The
   exit door closes when it powers down — open it with a turbine (or slip out
   before it shuts). Perks are dead while power is off; a turbine next to a perk
   machine powers it temporarily if you need to buy something.
2. Spin the mystery box until someone gets **EMP grenades**. This is pure RNG and
   can take rounds — start early.
3. Head to the **obelisk** in the cornfield between the farm and the power
   station (coming from the farm, the cornfield entrance is on the left before
   you reach power).
4. Hold the last zombie of the round under the obelisk and wait until the **sky
   fills with lightning**, then kill it. This biases the Avogadro to spawn at the
   obelisk — not guaranteed; repeat next round if it doesn't show.
5. When it spawns, **two players place turbines under the obelisk** (left and
   right of the NavCard table works well). Lure the Avogadro under the obelisk
   and **hit it with an EMP** — it flies up and charges the tower. Maxis confirms
   with a quote ("You have done well, but yet we need more energy").
6. **Two players take turbines to two different lampposts** and place them at the
   same time — the diner lamppost and the bus depot lamppost are the easiest
   pair. An orange orb appears at each lamp and connects to the obelisk: done.

**Troubleshooting:** both lamppost turbines must be down simultaneously and close
to the lamps. In a 4-player game, leave two turbines under the obelisk and use the
other two for the lamppost step. Once complete, you can turn the power back on —
it won't undo anything.

## Richtofen side (8:53)

Power stays **on** for this side. If someone is playing **Stuhlinger**, he hears
Richtofen quotes that confirm each step.

1. **Build the jet gun** (parts can be grabbed even before power). Craft at the
   bench in **town**. Spawn locations (9:26):
   - **Jet engine** — tunnel between bus depot and diner: between the cars past
     the lava pool, by the M16 wall buy, or left of the traffic cones.
   - **Handle** — inside **Nacht der Untoten** in the cornfield (right side of
     the road running farm → power, opposite the obelisk): filing cabinet by the
     lamp, the staircase corner, or the shelf.
   - **Wires** — power station catwalk past the switch room (drop through the
     gaping hole): on the generator, on the hallway barrel, or across the wooden
     board on the ground.
   - **Pressure gauge** — hunter's cabin in the woods between power and town
     (right-hand side): by the fireplace, on the bed, or on the table left of
     the bed.

   **Do not fire the jet gun to overheat yet** — if it breaks early, all four
   parts scatter and you rebuild from scratch.
2. **Everyone spins the box for EMP grenades** — as many players with EMPs as
   possible. At least one player also needs an **explosive weapon** (Ray Gun
   counts).
3. At the **obelisk**, deliberately **overheat the jet gun** until it breaks
   while standing underneath. Use a full charge (meter starting fully left) or
   the game may not register it. Richtofen quote confirms.
4. Get **25 explosive-weapon kills under the obelisk**. Richtofen quote confirms
   completion.
5. Every lamppost on the map now **glows green**, but only **four different
   lampposts** need an EMP — thrown at roughly the same time:
   - **4 players:** one lamp each. Easy.
   - **3 players:** one player takes both lamps at the **broken bridge** between
     town and bus depot (one on each side — quick enough to EMP both).
   - **2 players:** one player does the bridge pair; the other uses **Denizen
     portals** — lure a Denizen to a lamppost so it digs a teleporter, EMP that
     lamp, jump in the portal, and EMP the lamp on the other end immediately.
     You have roughly **10 seconds** total; failed lamps take ~60 seconds to
     turn green again.
6. The lamps glow **blue** and connect to the obelisk: done.

## Solo on PC (16:11)

With Plutonium + the solo mod (linked in the video description): the Richtofen
side only needs **two** lampposts EMP'd, and the Maxis side needs just **one**
turbine under the obelisk and **one** at a lamppost.

## After completion

The TranZit icon on the world map glows **orange** (Maxis) or **blue**
(Richtofen), and you can carry on to Die Rise or Buried. If you're chasing the
super easter egg, you'll come back here after Buried to build the NavCard bench —
that's covered in the creator's Buried guide.
