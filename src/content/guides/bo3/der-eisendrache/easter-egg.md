## Before you start

- **Players:** 1–4. Solo is fully possible.
- **You'll need:** at least one upgraded elemental bow per player (all four bows must be built across the team for the final steps on 4-player — solo you need one, but building multiple helps).
- **Rough length:** 1.5–3 hours depending on bow experience.

## Step 1 — Setup

1. Turn on power and open the map through to the pyramid room (undercroft).
2. Free the **Wrath of the Ancients** bow: feed three glowing dragon heads around the map (spawn wall, church, pyramid room) by letting them eat zombies.
3. Activate the pyramid: hold the buttons on both sides of the teleporter, then trap and kill zombies at the pyramid to fill soul canisters.

## Step 2 — Upgrade a bow

Each of the four elemental bows (Storm/Lightning, Fire, Void/Shadow, Wolf/Skull) has
its own multi-step upgrade quest starting from an arrow in the knight's hall. Pick one
and complete its quest — the **Storm bow** is the most popular for damage; the **Wolf
bow** is the easiest to run solo.

## Step 3 — The main quest

1. **Rocket test / Death Ray:** complete the rocket pad test sequence and use the Death Ray + teleporter steps to recover the **Keeper protector** items.
2. **Simon says:** activate the four pressure plates in the undercroft with the correct bow shots and complete the panel sequence in the lower lab.
3. **Soul boxes:** charge the floating boxes with kills where they appear around the map.
4. **The fireworks / rocket shield steps:** follow the quest chain through the rocket launch — shoot the rocket down at the right moment with the Ragnarok/bow steps as prompted.

## Step 4 — Boss fight

Teleport to the **Moon-side chamber** and defeat the Keeper/Dr. Groph encounter:
destroy the floating orbs he shields himself with, survive the zombie waves, and keep
feeding damage between shield phases. Bows + Ragnarok DG-4 slams carry this fight.

## Reward

Ending cutscene, achievement **My Brother's Keeper**, and bragging rights. On round
one-speedrun lobbies this is still one of the community's favorite quests ten years on.
