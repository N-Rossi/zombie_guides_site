## Why the stage

The stage is the biggest open area in Kino with clean sightlines, a wall weapon (MP40
downstairs feeds points early), the power switch, and the teleporter as a built-in
escape hatch. Zombies funnel in from predictable directions, which makes them easy
to group.

## Setup (rounds 1–10)

1. Points off the M14/Olympia in spawn for two rounds, then open toward the **MP40** (either route works; upstairs is cheaper overall).
2. Flip the **power**, buy **Juggernog** immediately.
3. Hit the box for the **Thundergun** — it's the whole strategy. Keep boxing (or take Ray Gun as a stopgap) until you have it.
4. Pack-a-Punch your point gun when you can; **Speed Cola** next, then Quick Revive (solo).

## The circuit

Run a loop: from the teleporter side of the stage, jog toward the power switch,
curl around the back of the stage, and swing wide past the front barriers. Zombies
collapse into a single trailing train. At the end of each loop:

- Turn, line the train up, and dump half a magazine into the lead zombies.
- Keep moving — never fully stop, sprint only to create a gap, not constantly.

## When it goes wrong

- Train too tight or you got clipped? **Thundergun** the horde and reset the loop.
- Truly cornered? **Teleporter.** You get a Pack-a-Punch room breather and drop back at spawn — run the long way back to the stage and restart the train.

## Round 30+

Bullets stop mattering in the 30s. Switch to Thundergun-only crowd clears, use the
**stage trap** and turret when they're off cooldown, and buy crawler insurance:
end each round with one slow zombie alive while you rebuild ammo off the carpenter/max
ammo cycle.
