## The spot

The rooftop by the church funnels spawns into a narrow approach, which turns
high rounds into a shooting gallery. With the **Thrustodyne M23 (Jet Gun)** built,
ammo stops being a problem — it recharges instead of reloading.

## Setup

1. Rush the power and Pack-a-Punch early; Liberty Falls is generous with points.
2. Build the **Jet Gun** (collect the parts around the map — water valve, electrical wires, tail section — and assemble at the workbench).
3. Perks: **Juggernog → Quick Revive → Speed Cola → PhD Flopper**, more as points allow. Vulture Aid's ammo drops are excellent here too.
4. Keep a **Mutant Injection** and/or self-revive from the crafting table as your oh-no button.

## The hold

Post up on the rooftop with your back to the parapet. Vacuum the funnel with the
Jet Gun, letting it vent before it overheats and breaks. A Pack-a-Punched wall gun
covers the vent windows. When a Manglers-heavy wave stacks up, pop the Mutant
Injection and clear the roof manually.

## Scaling

Past round 40, armor zombies and Manglers are the real threat — prioritize them the
moment they spawn, and keep salvage flowing into armor plates and injections. The
strategy is stable deep into the 50s+; beyond that it becomes an ammo-economy game.
