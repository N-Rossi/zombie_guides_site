## Overview

Der Riese ("The Giant") is the fourth and final World at War map, set in a Group 935
weapons facility in Breslau. It introduced two series staples: **Pack-a-Punch** and
**teleporters**. Small map, tight corners, hellhound rounds mixed in.

## Opening route

1. Buy the **Kar98k** off the wall if you need a crutch, but points come faster from knife kills in rounds 1–2.
2. Open the door to the right of spawn (toward Thompson) for the stronger wall buy.
3. Power is straight ahead through the courtyard — flip it as soon as round 4–5 economy allows.

## Perks

All four classics are here: **Juggernog**, **Quick Revive**, **Speed Cola**, and
**Double Tap**. Juggernog first, always.

## Pack-a-Punch and the teleporters

Pack-a-Punch sits in the spawn courtyard behind an energy gate. To open it:

1. Turn on the power.
2. Link each of the three teleporters (Z-A, Z-B, Z-C) by standing on the pad and holding the use key, then sprinting to the mainframe in spawn before the timer runs out.
3. Once all three are linked, the gate drops and Pack-a-Punch is yours — 5000 points per upgrade.

Link teleporters during hellhound rounds if you can: no zombies chasing you across the map.

## Worth knowing

- The **Monkey Bomb** makes its debut here — best tactical in the map for revives and teleporter links.
- The **fly trap** easter egg: shoot the switch on the balcony across from Teleporter Z-C with an upgraded weapon, then find and shoot/knife the three toys it scatters. Ends with "Samantha says goodnight."
- Hellhound rounds drop a Max Ammo on the final dog — don't reload until you grab it.
